# Accessibility

Horadric Odds should be usable by everyone who plays Diablo II: Resurrected, whatever device or assistive technology they use.

## What works today

- **Phones, tablets and desktops:** the layout adapts to small screens, and the site can be installed as an app and used offline.
- **Keyboard:** most controls are standard buttons, links and form fields, so they work with Tab and Enter. In the gear menu, the arrow keys move through items and Escape closes it.
- **Zoom:** text can be enlarged with your browser's zoom.
- **Languages:** English, Español, Português, Deutsch, 한국어 and 简体中文, chosen from the menu at the top right.
- **No time limits, sound or flashing content.**

## Known limitations

- Item tooltips appear on hover, or on tap on touch screens. Some details are only in tooltips.
- The skill trees, paper doll and inventory are built to look like the game. They are hard to use with a screen reader, and some icons have no text label.
- Some colours follow the game's own item colours (for example unique gold and set green), and a few of these have low contrast on the dark background.
- The build card made by **Share as image** is a picture, so its text isn't readable by screen readers. The share link and build code carry the same information as text.

## Reporting a barrier

If something stops you using the site, please tell us. [Open an issue](https://github.com/HoradricOdds/horadricodds.github.io/issues/new/choose) or email **HoradricOdds@hotmail.com**, and include the page, your device and browser, and any assistive technology you use. Barriers are treated as bugs.
