# Code of conduct

Horadric Odds is a free fan project for Diablo II: Resurrected players. This page sets out how we expect people to behave in its issues, discussions, pull requests and shared builds.

## The short version

Be kind, be patient and assume good faith. Everyone here is a player trying to help, whatever their language, skill level or how long they've played.

## Expected

- Be respectful, even when you disagree about a build, a number or a feature.
- Give useful detail and stay on topic.
- Accept that the maintainer decides what goes into the project, and why.
- Help newcomers. Many people reading the site are playing in their second language.

## Not acceptable

- Insults, harassment, threats, or personal or political attacks.
- Hate speech, or sexual, violent or discriminatory language or images.
- Sharing anyone's personal details, account names or contact information without their permission.
- Spam, advertising, trading or real-money sales, account sharing, or links to cheats, bots or malware.
- Posting other sites' guides or copyrighted material in full. Link to them instead.

## What happens

The maintainer may edit, hide or delete comments, issues, pull requests and shared builds that break these rules, and may block people who keep doing so. Decisions are made case by case.

## Reporting

Email **HoradricOdds@hotmail.com**, or use **Report** on a shared build on the site. Reports are kept private. Please include links or screenshots of what happened.

This code applies in the project's GitHub repository, in shared builds on the site, and in the Horadric Odds Discord bot.
